The spectrum

From Do It With Me to Do It For Me.

“Please just take care of all of this for me.”
Do It With MeYou drive. You spell out what you want.
Do It For MeYou hand it over. It may even suggest what’s next.
The spectrum

Not everything should be Do It For Me.

Stay in it · Do It With Me
  • Your users enjoy doing it
  • They’re learning and growing

AI should amplify them. Not replace them. It could be the point.

Hand it over · Do It For Me
  • It’s tedious
  • It’s clearly defined
  • Nobody wanted to do it anyway

Set it and forget it.

Not clearly defined yet? Keep them in the loop→Fill in what’s missing→Build up context→Fewer gaps over time

Pomelli · Before launch

Five questions to answer before you launch.

01What does done look like?A Business DNA with real fonts, colors and imagery. Images that are actually your product.
02Where do errors compound?Business DNA. It’s upstream of every photoshoot, campaign and website.
03What will users throw at it?Every kind of photo, every kind of business, every kind of website.
04What does a run cost?Every photoshoot is several generations. Cost scales with every click.
05How open is it?Photoshoot is narrow. The Pomelli Agent is a conversation.

Those five answers are the spec for your guardrails.

02 · Building the guardrails

Start with what your users want to do. Not the prompt.

01What users want to do
  • Open-ended chat: what questions will they ask?
  • Photoshoot: what photos will they upload?
  • Business DNA: what businesses do we test for?
02Functionality
  • Learn from what users do?
  • Run in the background, on cues?
  • Act, or only suggest?
03Interface
  • Where do I see what happened?
  • Where can I step in?
  • Where can I undo?

Clever prompting gets you a demo. Product-grade engineering gets you a launch.

Contracts and exits

Every step gets a contract. Every agent gets an exit.

Loop cap
15
Time budget
90s
Cost budget
$
Fallback
Retry. Go simpler. Or hand back something honest.

Worst case, an agent with no exit strategy just keeps going. Or breaks in unexpectedly bad ways.

Check every black box before it feeds the next one.

03 · Validating at scale

The loop

Break it. Bucket it. Guard it. Re-check it.

The one-off fix

Repeat until good enough. Good enough is a judgment call.
